Initial export lost seven frames; mixed JPEG/full-range and video/limited-range scenes caused filter reinitialization, and MP4 edit lists hid the last decoded scene frame. Normalize cinematic scene/crossfade output color metadata, clock and timebase, use constant frame rate and explicit concat shot durations, and validate exact output count. Repaired earlier normalized cache clips without changing their video packets, verifying decoded counts; rebuilt remaining scenes. Final benchmark independently probed: 1280x720 at 60 FPS, 2,939 encoded **and decoded** video frames; both MP4 streams start at zero and have duration 49.004667 s (about 21 ms above the requested 48.983333 s due to AAC priming / mux timing). Visual contact sheet of final video inspected. This is a recut of existing footage and recovered actual neural audio, not a new documentary or newly generated narration. A pure-silence input can cause FFmpeg loudnorm to output nonfinite samples; the attempted workaround was reverted, and this edge case remains open. The new mixed-source timing regression uses a non-silent fixture to isolate video clock behavior.
