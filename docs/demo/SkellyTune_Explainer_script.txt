Meet SkellyTune Mini. A compact vocal tuning plugin for your DAW. This is a conceptual demonstration.

Pitch correction estimates the vocal note, chooses an allowed target, and corrects toward that target.

Retune speed controls the response. Slower correction can preserve more of the original pitch movement.

Zero milliseconds retune removes correction smoothing. That does not mean zero plugin latency.

Choose a key and scale, or enable and disable individual notes using the interactive piano.

Compare your settings on actual vocal performances. Listen for musical expression and note transitions.
