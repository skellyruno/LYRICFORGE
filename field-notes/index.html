01 / SOUND00:45 · 60 FPS

The Shape of Sound

From the movement of air to the digital sample. A short visual exploration of vibration, waveforms and the frequencies that form a sound.

Film notes and text summary

Air particles vibrate around their resting positions. Pressure over time forms a waveform. A measured synthetic signal contains components at 440, 880 and 1320 Hz with normalized magnitudes 1, 0.5 and 0.25. A digital recording stores measurements at discrete moments. Music-led, without narration.

02 / IMAGE00:55 · 60 FPS

The Anatomy of an Image

A moving picture becomes a grid of measured colors, then a texture on a three-dimensional surface. The same information takes different forms.

Film notes and text summary

Moving pictures are sequences of frames. The genuine example capture contains 1440 × 900 pixels. A computed report measures its dimensions and RGB values. Averaged source colors become a texture, which wraps around changing geometry. Music-led, without narration.

03 / SILENCE00:27 · 60 FPS

The Architecture of Silence

Sound fades into silence. An original cinematic study of decay, measured signal envelopes and deliberate musical space.

Film notes and text summary

A synthetic 440 Hz floating-point tone is measured in 50 ms RMS windows. Its designed decay is 60 dB per second. This is a synthetic example, not a measurement of a room. A real silent interval is present in the final soundtrack. In digital silence, every PCM sample is zero. Music-led, without narration.

04 / COLOR00:41 · 60 FPS

The Science of Color

Light becomes information. Flowing simulations, schematic wavelengths and three channels of light build an image.

Film notes and text summary

Three example wavelengths are 420, 550 and 680 nm. Their display colors are illustrative. In an ideal additive RGB model, full-intensity red, green and blue combine to white. The animated dye is a stylized numerical flow, not a physical optical experiment. Music-led, without narration.
